Recent Company Updates (2024-2025)

October 2025

    Edify formed a strategic partnership with IIT Madras Research Park to create an AI research lab focused on healthcare analytics and drug discovery. The collaboration involves a ₹5 crore investment over 3 years and includes 10 PhD scholars.

    Edify won a ₹15 crore AI transformation contract from Tata Steel to deploy AI solutions like predictive maintenance and supply chain analytics in two steel plants, using over 500 IoT sensors, targeting ₹50 crore annual savings.

September 2025

    Edify received ISO 27001 certification for information security, assuring strong data protection and compliance with global standards.

    Launched EdgeAI platform for real-time industrial analytics with low latency and support for 20+ industrial protocols, aiming for 15% market share in India by 2027.

August 2025

    Opened first North American office in Toronto to serve pharmaceutical and healthcare clients with a specialized team, targeting $5 million revenue in 2026.

    Named "AI Company of the Year" by Kerala IT Mission for innovation, talent development, and client impact.

July 2025

    Completed Series A funding of ₹25 crores to expand operations, hire talent, and invest in R&D, led by Kalaari Capital and Blume Ventures.

    Partnered with Google Cloud in their AI/ML Acceleration Program, gaining access to cloud credits, technical support, and early AI tools.

June 2025

    Launched ‘AI for Good’ initiative providing ₹50 lakhs annually in pro bono AI and data science services to NGOs in healthcare, education, and environment sectors.

May 2025

    Edify’s clinical AI system received FDA Breakthrough Device designation for early sepsis detection with 95% accuracy, backed by trials at major Indian hospitals.
